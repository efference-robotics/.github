# Code of Conduct

We follow the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
It applies in every repository, issue, pull request and discussion of the organization.

To report unacceptable behaviour, contact an organization owner privately; the owners are listed on the organization's People page.
Reports are handled confidentially, and the enforcement guidelines of the Covenant apply.
